Spider-Man

Real Name: Peter Parker

Universe: Marvel

Role: Superhero

First Appearance: Amazing Fantasy #15

Abilities

Spider-Man has superhuman strength, speed, agility, spider-sense, wall-crawling abilities, and web-slinging skills.

Backstory

Peter Parker became Spider-Man after being bitten by a radioactive spider. He decided to use his powers to help others and protect people from danger.

Personality

Peter is intelligent, brave, responsible, and caring. He is also known for using humor while fighting villains.
